Help us compare six speed and battery experiments, especially on a Pixel. Open Nanogram, go to Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap the version line twice to open the Debug Menu, scroll down and find "Performance experiments". It shows your phone and six switches, all off. For each switch: test with it off, then turn it on (restart the app if it says so), and repeat the same thing: scroll a busy chat, take photos and videos, open GIFs, make a call. Note how smooth it felt (1 to 5), whether the phone got warm and how much battery went in 10 minutes, then send the diagnostics file (Settings > Help > Send diagnostics) right after each run. The full step-by-step guide is in the project's testing notes.
